1. What is blood donation?
Blood donation is voluntarily giving blood so it can be used for patients who need it.
2. What is the main organization responsible for blood transfusion services in Sri Lanka?
The National Blood Transfusion Service (NBTS).
3. Which ministry does NBTS operate under?
The Ministry of Health.
4. Where is the main NBTS center located?
The National Blood Centre is located in Narahenpita, Colombo 05.
5. Who receives blood supplied by NBTS?
Hospitals receive blood and blood components for patients who need them.
6. Is blood donation a paid service?
No. NBTS follows a voluntary non-remunerated blood donation system.
7. Who can benefit from blood donation?
Patients who need blood or blood components can benefit.
8. Can one blood donation help save a life?
Yes. Donated blood and its components can be used to treat patients.
9. Where are blood banks located in Sri Lanka?
Blood banks are located in hospitals throughout Sri Lanka.
10. Do I need to know a patient's name to donate blood?
No. You can donate voluntarily without knowing a specific patient.
11. Is donated blood given directly to a patient?
No. It is tested and processed before being used.
12. What is whole blood?
Whole blood contains the main components of blood together.
13. What are blood components?
They include red blood cells, platelets, plasma, and other components.
14. Does NBTS collect only whole blood?
No. It also provides other donation types, such as apheresis platelet donation.
15. What is platelet donation?
It is a donation where platelets are collected from the donor's blood.
16. Is stem cell donation the same as blood donation?
No. Stem cell donation is a separate donation process.
17. Why are blood donors not paid?
NBTS follows a voluntary, non-remunerated donation system.
18. What is the main purpose of NBTS?
To maintain a safe, quality and sufficient blood supply.
19. Where does NBTS mainly obtain blood from?
From voluntary blood donors.
20. Why is blood donation important?
It helps hospitals maintain the blood supply needed for patients.
21. What is the minimum age for blood donation?
18 years.
22. What is the usual maximum age for blood donation?
60 years.
23. What is the maximum age for a first-time donor?
55 years.
24. What is the minimum weight required?
More than 50 kg.
25. What should the hemoglobin level be?
It should be above 12.5 g/dL.
26. Can I donate blood every three months?
No. There should be at least a four-month interval between donations.
27. What if four months have not passed since my last donation?
You should wait until the required interval has passed.
28. Can a pregnant woman donate blood?
No.
29. Can someone with a serious illness donate blood?
They may not be eligible. The medical screening will determine eligibility.
30. Can every healthy person donate blood?
They must also meet all the required eligibility criteria.
31. Do I need to bring my NIC?
Yes. You should bring your NIC or another valid identification document.
32. Can I use my passport as identification?
Yes, a valid passport can be used as identification.
33. Can I use my driving licence?
Yes, a valid driving licence can be used as identification.
34. Can someone weighing less than 50 kg donate blood?
According to the NBTS basic criteria, the donor should weigh more than 50 kg.
35. What happens if my hemoglobin is low?
If it is not above 12.5 g/dL, you may not be eligible to donate.
36. If I do not have a serious illness, can I donate immediately?
Not necessarily. You must meet all other eligibility requirements too.
37. What are risk behaviours?
They are certain behaviours that may increase the risk to blood safety.
38. Can someone with risk behaviours donate blood?
Eligibility is determined through the NBTS medical screening process.
39. I am 59 years old. Can I donate blood?
You may be eligible based on age if you meet the other requirements.
40. I am 57 and want to donate for the first time. Can I donate?
No. The maximum age for a first-time donor is 55.
41. Should I eat before donating blood?
Yes. You should have a main meal within four hours before donation.
42. Can I donate blood on an empty stomach?
It is not recommended. You should have a main meal before donation.
43. Should I drink water before donating blood?
Yes. You should drink enough fluids.
44. Can I drink alcohol before donating blood?
No. Alcohol should be avoided.
45. How much sleep should I get before donating blood?
You should have at least six hours of rest the previous night.
46. Should I get enough rest before donation?
Yes.
47. Why should I drink enough water before donation?
It helps maintain proper hydration.
48. Why should alcohol be avoided before donation?
NBTS donor preparation guidelines advise donors to avoid alcohol.
49. Can I have breakfast before donating blood?
Yes.
50. What if I had a meal within four hours before donation?
That meets the recommended timing for having a main meal before donation.
51. What if I did not sleep well before donation?
Inform the staff at the donation center.
52. What if I feel unwell before donating blood?
Inform the medical staff before donating.
53. What if I am taking medication?
Tell the medical staff during the screening process.
54. Can I donate blood after travelling abroad?
It depends on the country you visited and the applicable deferral period.
55. Why is travel history important when donating blood?
Because travel to certain countries can affect blood donation eligibility.
56. What if I have travelled to a malaria-endemic country?
A deferral period may apply; NBTS guidance specifies a three-year deferral for listed malaria-endemic countries.
57. What if I travelled to another foreign country?
A deferral period may apply; NBTS guidance may specify three months depending on the travel circumstances.
58. Can I donate after travelling to India?
India is included in the malaria-endemic country information used by NBTS, so a deferral period may apply.
59. Can I donate after travelling to South Korea?
A deferral period may apply according to the current NBTS guidance.
60. Can I donate after travelling to Dubai/UAE?
Eligibility depends on the current NBTS travel criteria and should be confirmed with NBTS.
61. What if I do not remember my travel date?
Tell the donation center staff and ask them to confirm your eligibility.
62. Can I donate after the travel deferral period ends?
Yes, if you meet all the other eligibility requirements.
63. Can I hide my travel history when donating?
No. You should provide accurate information during screening.
64. Can the list of malaria-endemic countries change?
Yes. Therefore, you should check the current NBTS guidance.
65. What should I do if I am unsure about my travel eligibility?
Contact NBTS or your nearest blood bank for confirmation.
66. Can I donate blood at the National Blood Centre in Narahenpita?
Yes.
67. What are the donor opening hours at the National Blood Centre?
8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. every day.
68. Can I donate blood on Saturday?
Yes.
69. Can I donate blood on Sunday?
Yes.
70. What are the general opening hours of other blood banks?
Generally, 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
71. Can I find the nearest blood bank?
Yes. You can use the NBTS website to find a blood bank.
72. Can I donate at a mobile blood donation camp?
Yes.
73. Do I need to register before attending a mobile blood donation camp?
It depends on the specific camp. You should confirm with the organizer.
74. Are mobile blood donation camps held regularly?
Yes. Mobile donation campaigns play an important role in Sri Lanka's blood supply.
75. Can a mobile blood donation camp be cancelled?
Yes. A camp may be cancelled or rescheduled due to unavoidable circumstances.
76. What should I do before travelling to a distant mobile blood donation camp?
Confirm the camp details with the organizer or relevant blood bank.
77. Can I book a blood donation appointment?
Yes.
78. How can I make a Raktha appointment?
You can use the Raktha app, the official Raktha website, or contact a blood bank.
79. Can I visit a blood bank without an appointment?
This depends on the blood bank's procedure. Appointment facilities are also available.
80. What is the address of the National Blood Centre?
No. 555/5D, Elvitigala Mawatha, Narahenpita, Colombo 05.
81. Can I leave immediately after donating blood?
No. You should rest for at least 20 minutes.
82. Should I drink fluids after donating blood?
Yes. Drink more fluids during the next four hours.

Approximately 12 hours.
84. Can I do heavy lifting after donating blood?
No. Avoid heavy lifting for 24 hours.
85. Should I rest after donating blood?
Yes. Rest for at least 20 minutes.
86. Why should I drink fluids after donation?
To help maintain hydration.
87. Can I remove the plaster immediately?
It is recommended to keep it on for about 12 hours.
88. Can I do heavy work after donating blood?
Heavy lifting should be avoided for 24 hours.
89. Should I drink fluids for four hours after donation?
Yes. NBTS recommends drinking more fluids during the next four hours.
90. Should I follow the instructions given by the donation staff?
Yes. You should follow the post-donation instructions provided by the staff.
91. Is donated blood tested?
Yes. Required tests are performed to ensure blood safety.
92. Are blood components prepared from donated blood?
Yes.
93. Is blood group important?
Yes. Blood is managed according to the required blood groups.
94. Are there blood groups such as A, B, AB and O?
Yes.
95. What does Rh positive or Rh negative mean?
It refers to the Rh factor used in blood group classification.
96. Is the quality of donated blood checked?
Yes. NBTS monitors blood quality and safety.
97. Does NBTS take measures to keep blood safe?
Yes. Providing a safe and quality-assured blood supply is one of its main objectives.
98. Is blood stock monitored?
Yes. Blood bank stocks and related information are monitored.
99. Can I report a problem after blood donation?
Yes. You can provide feedback or report concerns to NBTS.
100. Where can I get more information about NBTS?
You can visit the official NBTS website or contact your nearest blood bank.
